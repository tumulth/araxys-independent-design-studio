## Users

- **Primary**: Venture-backed tech founders, challenger brands, and growth-stage executive leaders seeking transformative visual systems and digital experiences.
- **Secondary**: Elite design and engineering talent evaluating the studio for collaboration, plus industry curators seeking benchmark studio work.

## Product Purpose

The official digital flag and portfolio platform for **ARAXYS** (Independent Design Studio, based in Pune, India). Success means establishing undisputed design authority, immersing prospective clients in benchmark-grade creative execution, and driving high-conviction partnerships with ambitious companies ready to scale.

## Positioning

"We make brands impossible to ignore." While conventional agencies are slow and template-bound, ARAXYS offers high-velocity, sprint-based brand identity and digital experience execution—fusing visual engineering, cinematic motion, and uncompromising craft for founders with somewhere to go.

## Operating Context

- Evaluated by decision-makers during pitch cycles, fundraising milestones, and brand transformation initiatives.
- Accessed on both ultra-wide studio monitors and mobile touchscreens; demands instantaneous load times, responsive fluid motion, and zero visual friction.

## Product Principles

1. **Live as Proof**: The studio's own site must be the highest-fidelity artifact in the portfolio, proving capabilities through interaction rather than boastful copy.
2. **Speed & Rigor**: Sprint-driven momentum without compromising typographic precision, structural systems, or craft finish.
3. **Cinematic Depth**: Interface design is spatial and tactile; motion and depth transitions must feel physically integrated and purpose-built.
4. **Authority over Novelty**: Reject generic SaaS clichés, cookie-cutter templates, and gratuitous animations in favor of deliberate, editorial brand statements.
